{\Huge \textbf{Notation}}

\vspace{0.6cm}

A recurring difficulty in a text of this kind is that probabilists and practitioners
do not agree on the notations they use.  The table below fixes the necessary conventions. Very standard symbols or those introduced for local use only  are not included here.

$\PSpace$ & Complete probability space; $\PP$ is always the \emph{physical} measure.\\
$\QQ$ & Risk-neutral measure.\\
$\Filt$ & Filtration, always satisfying the usual conditions (Definition~\ref{def:usualconditions}).\\
$\FiltW$ & Augmented filtration generated by the Brownian motion $\Wt$.\\
$\E[\,\cdot\,]$, $\Ep{\QQ}[\,\cdot\,]$, $\Var[\,\cdot\,]$ & Expectation under $\PP$, and under a named measure. Variance.\\
$\Borel(E)$ & Borel $\sigma$-algebra of the topological space $E$.\\
$\Ind_A$ & Indicator function of the event $A$.\\
$\charf_X(u)$ & Characteristic function $\E[e^{iuX}]$.\\

\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Processes}}\\
\hline
$\Wt$ & Brownian motion. \\
$\Wvec$ & Multidimensional Brownian motion with independent components.\\
$\BH$ & Fractional Brownian motion with Hurst parameter $\Hurst$.\\
$\Hurst$ & Hurst parameter, always.\\
$\mu$ & The \emph{drift} of a process, or the mean of a distribution.\\
$\sigma$ & A \emph{diffusion coefficient}; $\Sigma=\sigma\sigma\transp$ is the diffusion matrix.\\
$\rho$ & A \emph{correlation}, always; $P=(\rho_{ij})$ is a correlation matrix (Definition~\ref{def:correlatedbm}).\\
$p_{s,x}(t,y)$, $p_T$ & Transition density of a Markov process, and the terminal density of $\Spot_T$.\\
$V_1(f)_t$, $V_p(f)_t$ & Total variation and $p$-variation of $f$ on $[0,t]$.\\
$\rqv{X}$, $\qv{X}$ & Realised (pathwise) and predictable quadratic variation of $X$, respectively.\\
$\Lad{[0,T]\times\Om}$ & Adapted integrands square-integrable in $dt\otimes d\PP$.\\
$\Lloc{\Om,L^p[0,T]}$ & The larger, pathwise class: adapted, with $\int_0^T|f|^p\,dt<\infty$ almost surely.\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Finance}}\\
\hline
$\Spot_t$ & Spot price of the underlying.\\
$\Fwd_t$ or $\Fwd(t,T)$ & Forward price for delivery at $T$.\\
$\Bond{t}{T}$, $\Disc{t}{T}$ & Price at $t$ of a zero-coupon bond maturing at $T$, and the discount factor from $T$ back to $t$.\\
$\Num$ & Numeraire asset.\\
$\Payoff_T$, $\PayoffFn$ & Terminal payoff (a random variable) and payoff function.\\
$\Strike$ & Strike price; $\lmon=\log(\Strike/\Fwd)$ is log-moneyness.\\
$\rf$, $\divy$ & Short rate and continuous dividend (or repo) yield.\\
$\ivol$, $\lvol$, $\rvol$ & Implied, local and realised volatility; $\tvar=\ivol^2\,T$ is total implied variance.\\
$V$ & The value of a derivative.\\
$\pi$, $\Pi_t$ & A trading strategy, and the value of a hedging portfolio.\\
$N(\cdot)$ & Standard normal distribution function.\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Rough paths}}\\
\hline
$\rpX=(X,\lev)$ & A rough path: a trajectory \emph{plus} a second level it does not determine.\\
$\lev_{s,t}$, $\levW_{s,t}$ & That second level, a function of \emph{two} times and never a path.\\
$A_{s,t}$ & L\'evy area: the antisymmetric part of $\lev_{s,t}$, its only free datum for a pair.\\
$\alpha$ & H\"older exponent of a rough path, always in $(\tfrac13,\tfrac12]$; distinct from $\Hurst$.\\
$\Sig(\rpX)$, $\Sig^{N}$ & Signature of a rough path, and its truncation at level $N$.\\
\hline
